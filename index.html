    About Me!

    Elizabeth is a Junior in Farmington High-school. To date Elizabeth's greatest technical accomplishment is her consistent hard-work in her journey with technology. Elizabeth's tech journey started at a very young age with block-coding with Scratch, she was fascinated with expressing her creativity with code. In Freshman year of high-school she used online resources to learn how to make websites using HTML and CSS, she learned how to code in Python, and she taught herself how to 3D modeling with blender. As a Sophomore Elizabeth took AP Computer Science Principles, receiving a 5 in the AP test and winning an award with NCWIT. Today Elizabeth continues pursuing her passion of technology, she is currently taking AP Computer Science A with Java in school, and she has a paid internship at TheCoderSchool teaching young children a variety of skills in technology. In her future Elizabeth wants to combine her passion for both STEM and creativity to create more original creative works. She wants to continue learning computer science and she hopes to go to Yale in the future.
