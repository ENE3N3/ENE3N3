    About Me!

    My name is Kati and I'm a game artist based in Finland! I sell at artist alleys on the side, any info about my next event you'll find on my instagram!

    I'm passionate about making games with characters and stories that people love. I'm currently working on a multiplayer social deduction game called Ratacombs. If you like the sound of silly medieval rats, join our discord to follow our development!

    Themes I enjoy exploring are love, queer experience, the self, multiculturalism, and humor. Little critters are also a favorite of mine to illustrate.
